The cost of waiting

Saving longer can still leave the goal moving away from you.

While you wait, you may keep paying rent and trying to build a larger deposit. If home values also rise, the amount you need can keep moving too.

BuySooner is designed to help eligible buyers enter earlier and start participating in home ownership sooner.

Good questions

The things buyers usually want to know first.

Do I own the home?

Yes. You are the legal owner from settlement.

Do I make monthly repayments to BuySooner?

No regular monthly BuySooner repayment is required during the term. You continue making your normal home-loan repayments.

How does BuySooner get repaid?

BuySooner is repaid later under the agreed structure, usually through refinance, with sale also available as an exit pathway.

Is BuySooner permanent?

No. It is designed as temporary capital, generally with a refinance or sale pathway over roughly three to five years.
